HOW TO PLAY

Use Left & Right Button To Move The Car. Use Different Kinds Of Super Powers.

1. Nitro Boost: Speed Up Your Vehicle For 5 Seconds.
2. Fire Button: The Car Starts Firing For 5 Seconds.

Play And Collect Coins. In Future Updates Ill Add Purchase Option.

You Can Choose Different Colour Vehicles & Maps From Shop.
